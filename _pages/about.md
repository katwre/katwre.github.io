I’m a Zurich-based data scientist with over nine years of experience in machine learning, biomedical research, and software development. On this personal website, I share insights from my academic and industry work in data analysis, disease (epi)genomics, and tool development.

I trained in computer science and bioinformatics at the [University of Warsaw’s Faculty of Mathematics, Informatics and Mechanics (MIMUW)](https://www.mimuw.edu.pl/en/). I completed my Ph.D. as a doctoral researcher in Prof. Altuna Akalin’s group at the [Bioinformatics and Omics Data Science Platform at MDC-BIMSB](https://www.mdc-berlin.de/bioinformatics), where I co-developed computational methods, the BioC [genomation](https://github.com/BIMSBbioinfo/genomation) package, and the [PiGx BS-seq](https://github.com/BIMSBbioinfo/pigx) workflow for disease epigenomics. This included integrating multiple NGS datasets to investigate [technical biases in ChIP-seq](https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkz460). My research also examined epigenomic changes in neuroblastoma and [cardiovascular disease](https://doi.org/10.1093/nargab/lqad061) using solid tissue and liquid-biopsy samples from blood and urine. At ETH Zurich ([Laboratory of Nutrition and Metabolic Epigenetics](https://epigenetics.ethz.ch/), [Laboratory of Regenerative and Muscle Biology](https://rmb.ethz.ch/)) and the [Functional Genomics Center Zurich](https://fgcz.ch/the-center/people/rehrauer.html) I further explored scRNA-seq analysis, including germline chimerism.


In industry and freelance collaborations, I have applied machine learning and deep learning to translational challenges, including prioritizing therapeutic targets for clinical trials, developing single-cell RNA-seq methods for endometriosis prediction, monitoring wastewater for chemical anomalies, and identifying blood-based biomarkers for cancer and autoimmune diseases. **Research interests**

Rapid advances in molecular profiling create new opportunities to study disease through genomics, epigenomics, and transcriptomics - including bulk, single-cell, and spatially resolved approaches - as well as imaging and proteomics. I integrate these data using machine learning and deep learning to address biological and clinical questions. I am committed to advancing machine learning research in biomedicine to improve clinical decision-making.
